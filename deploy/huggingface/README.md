# Stampa — public demo

A large Nigerian buyer cannot deduct input VAT on an invoice without a
government reference number. Stampa is the product the buyer pays for and the
supplier is delivered to by their own customer.

This Space is a **simulated** instance. Reference numbers are not real tax
records. Open the app, pick a door, and click through.

Source and the decision memo: [github.com/moyesrex-ops/New-agent-work](https://github.com/moyesrex-ops/New-agent-work/pull/1)
