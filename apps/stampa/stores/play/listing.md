# Google Play listing — Stampa

**Title:** Stampa

**Short description:**
Get the NRS reference your customer can pay. Free for suppliers.

**Full description:**
From July 2026 a buyer in Nigeria can only pay an invoice that carries an NRS reference number. Stampa gets that number for you.

Your customer sends a WhatsApp link. You confirm the details they already hold. You send the invoice. The Nigeria Revenue Service returns a reference anyone can check at nrs.gov.ng.

Stampa is free for suppliers. You will never be asked to pay. Bank details come from your customer and cannot be changed in the app.
